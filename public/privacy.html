RDMC Fairfield County Live

Privacy policy

RDMC Fairfield County uses information about members to operate the chapter app and manage access to chapter features.

Accounts and membership

The app uses Supabase for authentication and storage. Account records include email address, user ID, name, chapter, country, role, access scope and subscription status. A chapter administrator can maintain directory entries with names and phone numbers.

Chapter activity

When members post messages, the app stores the message, author ID and time and displays it in the members chat. Chapter tools also store announcements, events, tasks, meeting details and member-linked consumption entries, including products, quantities, dates and optional notes. Access depends on the feature and the member’s permissions.

Optional features

Voice commands use the speech recognition available on the device or in its browser. Confirming a dictated message posts it to members chat. Opening a meeting or external service takes you to that provider.

The background-check feature can direct members to a screening provider. The app is designed to store provider identifiers, screening status and a permitted summary for chapter administration.

Privacy requests

To ask about your data, correct account information or request deletion, contact the chapter administrator through app support.
