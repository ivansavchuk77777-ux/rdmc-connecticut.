App support

For help signing in, checking your membership approval, reporting an issue, or requesting account deletion, email the chapter administrator at rdmcfairfieldcounty@gmail.com.

Use the email address associated with your RDMC account. A chapter administrator can check your approval status and chapter access.

Report a problem

Include your device model, app version, and the screen where the issue occurred. Keep passwords, verification codes, private conversations and background-check documents out of the request.
